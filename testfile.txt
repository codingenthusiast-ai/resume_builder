Water scarcity is a growing challenge that affects communities around the world. It occurs when the demand for clean, usable water exceeds the available supply. Climate change, prolonged droughts, pollution, population growth, and inefficient water use all contribute to this problem. When water is scarce, families struggle to meet basic needs, farmers cannot grow enough food, and ecosystems suffer as rivers and wetlands dry up.

Addressing water scarcity requires both individual action and coordinated planning. Repairing leaks, using water-efficient appliances, harvesting rainwater, and adopting efficient irrigation methods can reduce waste. Communities and governments can protect water sources, improve water infrastructure, and treat wastewater for safe reuse. By conserving water and managing it fairly, we can help ensure that everyone has access to this essential resource now and in the future.
